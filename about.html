ABOUT

Spice Haven was born in 2010 from a simple belief — that great Indian food should be accessible, authentic, and deeply nourishing[cite: 6]. Founded by Chef Arjun Mehta and his family, our kitchen draws on recipes passed down through three generations, refined with the finest local produce and spices sourced directly from India[cite: 6].

Every dish on our menu tells a story — of regions, rituals, and the remarkable diversity of Indian cuisine[cite: 6]. From the slow-braised kormas of the north to the coastal seafood curries of Kerala, we bring the full spectrum of flavour to your table[cite: 6].

We are proud to have served over 50,000 guests in the heart of San Francisco, earning a 4.9-star rating and recognition as one of the Bay Area's top Indian restaurants three years running[cite: 6]. Our doors are always open — come discover your new favourite dish[cite: 6].
